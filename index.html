Why Bharat eVote

Election infrastructure that feels effortless

Everything an organisation needs to run a credible vote — from eligibility rules to publication — in one clean, auditable platform.

Anonymous by design

Participation is recorded separately from the ballot itself. Administrators can prove turnout without ever seeing how an individual voted.

One voter, one vote

A unique database constraint and an atomic server-side transaction reject duplicate ballots, even when two requests arrive at the same instant.

Tamper-evident audit trail

Administrative actions are appended to a hash-chained log, so edits to election settings or voter status can be reviewed later.

Configurable eligibility

Set who can vote per election, approve or suspend accounts, and control whether verification is required before a ballot is accepted.

Built for India, ready to localise

English and Hindi ship today, with a dictionary structure that makes adding more Indian languages straightforward.

Transparent analytics

Turnout, participation trends and candidate shares visualised with Chart.js — published only when results are officially released.

Security & transparency

Trust is a technical feature, not a promise

Ballots are decoupled from identity at the database level. Administrators manage elections and eligibility — never individual choices. Every sensitive operation runs through server-side validation, and public results stay hidden until they are officially published.

Educational prototype. Bharat eVote is not affiliated with the Government of India or the Election Commission. No Aadhaar, blockchain or official certification is claimed.
Explore the voter dashboard

Separated participation and ballots

A participation row proves you voted; a standalone ballot row holds the choice with no voter reference.

Row Level Security

PostgreSQL policies scope every read and write. Administrative functions are protected server-side, never by hidden UI alone.

Append-only audit log

Each entry carries the previous entry's hash, making silent history edits detectable during review.

Time-bound elections

Ballots are accepted only inside the configured window, verified again on the server for every submission.
